Reading the map

Click a component to highlight shared parts. Click a method name to reveal its formula. Unoutlined rows show typical recipes; dashed cards show alternatives.

Offline uses training data prepared before fitting; it may involve multiple epochs. Online refreshes training data as the model changes. Online/offline describe data collection; on-policy/off-policy describe the sampling policy. Dₖ is the dataset refreshed in round k.

The online SFT and DPO alternatives need fresh labels or preference pairs. Online RFT repeatedly generates and filters fresh actor responses. Online distillation here uses student-generated prefixes with detached sampling: teacher token labels for hard distillation, direct KL matching (GKD) for soft distillation.

The reward axis refers to explicit response scores. RFT uses them to filter responses; PPO, GRPO, and CISPO use them to update the policy. DPO uses supplied preference pairs. The Reward switch selects a correctness Verifier or Reward LLM.

Blue blocks are LLM-based components. The stronger blue Actor LLM is the trainable policy (the student in distillation); the final loss → Actor LLM arrow means a weight update. Reward and critic models use scalar output heads.
