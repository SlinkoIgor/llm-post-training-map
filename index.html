Reading the map

Click a component to highlight the same part in other methods. Click a method name to reveal its formula.

Blue blocks are LLM-based components. The stronger blue Actor LLM is the trainable policy πθ (the student in distillation); the final loss → Actor LLM arrow means a weight update. Reward and critic models use scalar output heads. The Reward switch selects a learned reward model or a correctness verifier for RFT, PPO, GRPO, and CISPO.

The reward axis refers to explicit response scores from a correctness check or Reward LLM. RFT uses scores to filter responses; PPO, GRPO, and CISPO use them to update the policy. DPO uses supplied preference pairs; SFT and distillation use supplied or teacher targets. Placements describe common recipes. Off-policy means external responses; offline means a fixed dataset. PPO, GRPO, and CISPO sample recent policy snapshots and correct drift with importance ratios.

Hard distillation matches teacher tokens with CE; soft distillation matches teacher distributions with KL. On-policy hard distillation uses teacher labels on student-generated prefixes; on-policy soft distillation uses direct KL matching (GKD). Both detach the sampling step. Policy-gradient distillation is another variant.
